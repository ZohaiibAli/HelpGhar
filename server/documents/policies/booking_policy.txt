HelpGhar - Booking Policy

OVERVIEW
This policy explains how service bookings work on HelpGhar, from selecting
a worker to confirming a completed job.

HOW BOOKING WORKS
1. Customers browse workers by service category and city.
2. Only verified workers with approved accounts appear in search results.
3. The customer reviews the worker's profile, ratings, pricing, and
   availability, then selects a date, start time, and expected duration.
4. HelpGhar checks that time slot for conflicts. If the worker already has
   an active booking at that time, the slot is blocked and cannot be
   double-booked.
5. Once the slot is confirmed, HelpGhar sends a booking confirmation to
   both the customer and the worker, including the service type, date,
   time, and contact details.

TIME SLOTS
A selected time slot is temporarily locked while the booking is being
completed, so two customers cannot book the same worker for the same time.
If payment or confirmation is not completed within the checkout window, the
slot is released back for other customers.

MODIFYING A BOOKING
Customers can reschedule a booking to a different available time slot,
subject to the cancellation policy's cutoff window. Workers are notified
automatically whenever a booking is modified.

BOOKING HISTORY
Every customer and worker has access to a full booking history from their
dashboard, including completed, cancelled, and upcoming services. This
record is kept for transparency and can be referenced in the event of a
dispute.

WORKER RESPONSIBILITIES
Workers are expected to update job status (Started, In Progress, Completed)
in real time so the customer can track progress. A job is only marked
"Completed" after the customer confirms it, which protects customers from
false reporting.

WHAT TO DO IF A WORKER DOES NOT SHOW UP
If a verified worker fails to arrive for a confirmed booking, the customer
should report the issue through the dispute form. HelpGhar will investigate
and may issue a refund, reassign the booking, or take action against the
worker's account depending on the outcome.
