HelpGhar - Watchmen

WHAT WATCHMEN OFFER
HelpGhar watchmen (security guards) provide on-site security for homes
and residential compounds, including monitoring entry points, basic
patrol rounds, and reporting suspicious activity.

ARRANGEMENT TYPES
Watchmen are typically booked on a monthly basis for ongoing shifts
(day, night, or 24-hour rotations depending on the individual worker's
availability). Check the price unit and shift details on each worker's
profile.

WHAT'S TYPICALLY INCLUDED
- Monitoring the property's entry/exit points
- Basic patrol of the premises during their shift
- Logging visitors as needed
- Reporting any security concerns promptly

WHAT TO DISCUSS BEFORE BOOKING
Confirm shift timing, whether the role is stationary or includes
patrol duties, and any specific property layout details the watchman
should be aware of.

VERIFICATION
Given the trust involved in security roles, always check for the
CNIC-verified badge and review a watchman's experience and any listed
references or certificates before hiring.

BOOKING A WATCHMAN
Search for "Watchmen" under service categories, filter by city and
budget, and compare verified profiles by rating and experience before
booking.
