HelpGhar - Electrician Services

COMMON JOBS HANDLED
HelpGhar electricians typically handle wiring repairs and installation,
switchboard and socket replacement, fan and light fixture installation,
MCB and circuit breaker issues, short-circuit troubleshooting, and general
electrical fault-finding in homes.

WHAT'S USUALLY INCLUDED IN A CALL-OUT
A standard electrician visit includes diagnosing the reported issue, minor
repairs (such as replacing a switch, socket, or fuse), and basic safety
checks on the affected circuit. Larger jobs, like rewiring a full room or
installing new wiring for an extension, are usually quoted separately once
the electrician has assessed the site.

SAFETY AND VERIFICATION
All electricians listed on HelpGhar go through CNIC verification, and many
also upload trade certificates that appear on their profile. Look for the
"CNIC Verified" badge and check the worker's experience and rating before
booking, especially for larger electrical jobs.

PRICING
Electricians are typically priced per hour or per visit. Simple call-outs
(a single switch or socket repair) are usually on the lower end of a
worker's listed price range, while larger jobs (full-room wiring, panel
upgrades) are priced higher and may require an on-site quote.

WHAT TO HAVE READY
It helps to know your electrical panel's location, describe the exact
issue (e.g. "socket not working," "lights flickering," "tripped MCB"), and
ensure the affected area is accessible before the electrician arrives.

EMERGENCY ISSUES
For serious hazards such as sparking outlets, burning smells, or exposed
live wires, turn off the relevant circuit breaker if it is safe to do so
and book the earliest available verified electrician, noting the urgency
in your booking request.

BOOKING AN ELECTRICIAN
Search for "Electrician" under service categories, filter by city and
availability, and compare verified profiles by rating and price before
scheduling a time slot.
