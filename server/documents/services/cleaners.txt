HelpGhar - Cleaning Services

TYPES OF CLEANING OFFERED
HelpGhar cleaners offer standard cleaning, deep cleaning, and move-in /
move-out cleaning. Some workers also offer specialised services like sofa
and carpet shampooing on request.

WHAT STANDARD CLEANING INCLUDES
Standard cleaning typically covers sweeping and mopping floors, dusting
visible surfaces, wiping down kitchen counters, cleaning bathroom fixtures,
emptying trash bins, and general tidying of living areas. It's meant for
regular upkeep of an already-maintained home.

WHAT DEEP CLEANING INCLUDES
Deep cleaning goes beyond the standard routine and typically covers:
- Detailed scrubbing of kitchen surfaces, including behind appliances
  where accessible, stovetops, and cabinet exteriors
- Bathroom deep-clean including grout, tiles, and fixtures
- Wiping down doors, door frames, switchboards, and skirting boards
- Cleaning inside and under furniture where accessible
- Window sills and ledges
- Cobweb removal from ceilings and corners
- Detailed floor scrubbing and mopping

Deep cleaning takes longer than a standard clean and is priced and
scheduled accordingly on each worker's profile.

WHAT IS TYPICALLY NOT INCLUDED
Exterior window cleaning, wall painting touch-ups, pest control, laundry
and ironing, and heavy furniture moving are generally not included unless
a specific worker's profile states otherwise. Always confirm exact scope
with the worker before booking if you have specific requirements.

PRICING
Cleaning services on HelpGhar are usually priced per visit or per hour,
depending on the worker's listed pricing unit. Deep cleaning is generally
priced higher than a standard clean due to the additional time and effort
involved.

WHAT TO PREPARE BEFORE THE CLEANER ARRIVES
Customers are encouraged to put away valuables and clear clutter from
surfaces so the cleaner can work efficiently, and to communicate any
specific areas of focus at the start of the job.

BOOKING A CLEANER
Search for "Cleaner" or "Maid" under service categories, filter by your
city and preferred budget, and review verified profiles with ratings
before booking a time slot.
