HelpGhar - Cooks

WHAT COOKS OFFER
HelpGhar cooks prepare home meals, ranging from everyday Desi cooking
to specialised cuisines depending on the individual worker's skills.
Many list their specialities (e.g. Desi, BBQ, continental, baking) on
their profile.

ARRANGEMENT TYPES
Cooks are typically booked on a monthly basis for regular daily meal
preparation, though some are available for hourly or event-based
cooking. Check the price unit on each worker's profile.

WHAT'S TYPICALLY INCLUDED
- Daily meal planning and preparation based on customer preference
- Basic kitchen cleanup after cooking
- Grocery list suggestions (grocery shopping itself is usually a
  separate arrangement unless stated otherwise)

DIETARY REQUIREMENTS
If you have specific dietary needs (vegetarian, low-oil, diabetic-
friendly, etc.), discuss this with the cook before booking to confirm
they're comfortable preparing meals accordingly.

VERIFICATION
Check for the CNIC-verified badge on a cook's profile, and review any
listed certificates (e.g. culinary training) alongside their rating
and reviews before hiring.

BOOKING A COOK
Search for "Cooks" under service categories, filter by city and
budget, and compare verified profiles by rating, specialty, and
experience before booking.
