HelpGhar - Baby Sitters

WHAT BABY SITTERS OFFER
HelpGhar baby sitters provide childcare for infants, toddlers, and
young children, including feeding, supervised play, nap-time routines,
and basic hygiene care. Some also assist with homework or light
educational activities for older children.

ARRANGEMENT TYPES
Baby sitters can be booked hourly for occasional childcare, or on a
monthly basis for ongoing daily care. Check the price unit on each
worker's profile.

WHAT'S TYPICALLY INCLUDED
- Feeding and basic meal preparation for the child
- Supervised play and activities
- Nap-time and bedtime routines
- Basic hygiene care (diapering, bathing, as age-appropriate)
- Light homework help for school-age children

SAFETY AND VERIFICATION
Given the sensitivity of childcare work, always check for the
CNIC-verified badge and review a baby sitter's rating and past reviews
carefully before booking. Some baby sitters also list first-aid or
childcare-related certificates on their profile.

WHAT TO DISCUSS BEFORE BOOKING
Before confirming, it helps to share your child's age, any allergies
or medical needs, daily routine, and specific expectations so the baby
sitter can prepare accordingly.

BOOKING A BABY SITTER
Search for "Baby Sitters" under service categories, filter by city and
budget, and compare verified profiles by rating and experience before
booking.
