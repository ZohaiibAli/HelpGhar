HelpGhar - Worker Verification Policy

WHY WORKERS ARE VERIFIED
HelpGhar verifies every worker before they can accept bookings, so
customers can trust the identity and stated skills of the people they
invite into their homes.

WHAT WORKERS MUST SUBMIT
During registration, workers upload a copy of their CNIC (national
identity card) and any certificates relevant to their service category
(for example, an electrical trade certificate, a teaching credential, or a
driving license). These documents are stored securely and are only
accessible to the HelpGhar admin team.

HOW VERIFICATION WORKS
1. The worker submits their registration along with CNIC and certificates.
2. The admin dashboard receives an automatic notification of the new
   request.
3. An admin reviews the submitted documents.
4. The admin can approve the account, reject it, or request additional
   details or resubmission.
5. The worker is notified automatically of the outcome.

WHAT "CNIC VERIFIED" MEANS ON A PROFILE
A "CNIC Verified" badge on a worker's profile means their identity document
has been reviewed and confirmed by the HelpGhar admin team. Only verified
workers appear in customer search results and are eligible to receive
bookings.

REJECTED OR INCOMPLETE APPLICATIONS
If an application is rejected or documents are unclear, the worker is
notified with the reason and can resubmit corrected information. Accounts
remain restricted from taking bookings until verification is approved.

ONGOING TRUST AND SAFETY
Even after verification, the admin team can review, suspend, or restrict a
worker's account in response to serious complaints, repeated cancellations,
or policy violations, as part of the platform's ongoing dispute-handling
process.

PASSWORD AND DATA SECURITY
All account passwords are stored in encrypted form, and financial and
identity data is protected with role-based access control so only
authorised admin staff can view sensitive records.
