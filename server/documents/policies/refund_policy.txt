HelpGhar - Refund Policy

WHEN REFUNDS ARE ISSUED
Refunds are issued when a booking is cancelled within the allowed window,
when a worker fails to complete a service, or when the admin approves a
refund as part of resolving a dispute (for example, poor service quality
or a no-show).

HOW REFUNDS ARE PROCESSED
Approved refunds are processed automatically through the same payment
method originally used for the transaction (debit/credit card or digital
wallet). Refund details, including amount and reason, are logged for
transparency and can be viewed in the customer's transaction history.

PARTIAL REFUNDS
In some dispute cases, the admin may approve a partial refund rather than
a full refund, for example when part of a service was completed
satisfactorily. The reasoning for a partial refund is shared with the
customer when the decision is made.

COMMISSION ON REFUNDS
If a refund is issued before a service is completed, HelpGhar's platform
commission for that transaction is also reversed. If a service was partly
delivered, commission handling is decided as part of the dispute
resolution.

REFUND TIMELINE
Once a refund is approved, it is initiated immediately on the HelpGhar
side. The time it takes to appear in the customer's account depends on the
customer's bank or wallet provider, and is generally outside HelpGhar's
direct control.

HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND
Refund requests are made through the dispute form, referencing the
relevant booking. Provide as much detail as possible (what went wrong, and
any evidence such as messages or photos) so the admin can review the case
quickly and fairly.

NON-REFUNDABLE SITUATIONS
Bookings that were completed and confirmed by the customer, or
cancellations made after the applicable cancellation cutoff, are generally
not eligible for a refund unless the admin determines there was a valid
service issue.
