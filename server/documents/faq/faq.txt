HelpGhar - Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT IS HELPGHAR?
HelpGhar is an online marketplace that connects customers in Pakistan with
verified domestic and skilled workers, including electricians, plumbers,
cleaners, maids, tutors, carpenters, mechanics, painters, AC technicians,
and drivers. Every worker on the platform goes through a CNIC and
certificate verification process before they are allowed to accept bookings.

HOW DO I CREATE AN ACCOUNT?
You can register as either a Customer or a Worker from the HelpGhar
registration page. You will be asked for your name, email address, contact
number, and a password. Workers additionally upload their CNIC and any
relevant skill certificates during registration so the admin team can
verify their identity and qualifications.

HOW LONG DOES VERIFICATION TAKE?
After a worker submits their registration with CNIC and certificates, the
admin reviews the documents and either approves, rejects, or requests
additional details. Approved workers receive a notification and can log in
immediately. Verification is manual, so exact timing can vary, but workers
are notified as soon as a decision is made.

CAN I USE HELPGHAR BEFORE MY ACCOUNT IS VERIFIED?
No. Unverified accounts cannot access booking, service listings, or
messaging features. This restriction exists to keep the platform safe and
trustworthy for both customers and workers.

HOW DO I FIND A WORKER?
Browse services by category (for example, Electrician, Plumber, Cleaner,
Tutor) and filter by city, budget, or experience. Only verified workers
with approved accounts appear in search results. Each worker profile shows
their rating, reviews, pricing, and availability so you can compare before
booking.

HOW IS PRICING DETERMINED?
Workers set their own price range and the unit it applies to (per hour,
per day, or per month, depending on the service). The price shown on a
worker's profile is the range you should expect to pay; the exact amount
can be confirmed with the worker before the job starts.

IS MY PAYMENT SECURE?
Yes. HelpGhar processes payments through secure, SSL-encrypted payment
gateways and supports debit/credit cards and digital wallets. The
platform's commission is deducted automatically, and the remaining amount
is held until the customer confirms the job is complete, at which point it
is released to the worker.

WHAT HAPPENS IF I HAVE A COMPLAINT?
You can submit a complaint through the dispute form in your account,
describing the issue and referencing the relevant booking. The admin team
reviews every complaint, communicates with both parties if needed, and can
issue refunds or warnings as appropriate. All disputes are logged with a
status (Pending, In Review, Resolved) so you can track progress.

CAN I RATE A WORKER?
Yes. After a service is marked complete, you will be prompted to rate the
worker on punctuality, behaviour, and quality of service, and you can leave
a written review. This feedback contributes to the worker's average rating
and helps other customers choose the right person for the job.

DOES HELPGHAR OPERATE IN MY CITY?
HelpGhar currently lists workers across major Pakistani cities including
Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, and
Quetta. Availability of specific service categories can vary by city.

HOW DO I CONTACT SUPPORT?
If your question isn't covered here or in the chat assistant, you can reach
HelpGhar support through the contact options listed on the website, or
submit a dispute/support request from your account dashboard.
