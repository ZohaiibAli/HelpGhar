HelpGhar - Home Teachers

WHAT HOME TEACHERS OFFER
HelpGhar home teachers offer home tuition across school and college
levels, including Matric, O-Levels, A-Levels, and general subject
tutoring such as Mathematics, Science, English, and Computer Studies.
Home Teacher profiles list their subject specialisation and experience
so you can match a home teacher to your child's exact needs.

HOW HOME TUTORING WORKS
Once you book a home teacher, sessions are typically scheduled on a
recurring basis (for example, a fixed number of days per week), with
the price shown on the home teacher's profile usually reflecting a
per-month or per-hour rate, depending on how the home teacher has
listed it.

COMPARING HOME TEACHER RATES
Rates vary based on subject, grade level, and the home teacher's
experience and qualifications. O-Level and A-Level home teachers, or
subject specialists (e.g. Mathematics, Physics), are generally priced
higher than general primary or middle-school tuition. Always compare a
few verified profiles by rating, experience, and price range before
choosing.

VERIFICATION FOR HOME TEACHERS
Home Teachers go through the same CNIC verification as other HelpGhar
workers, and many also list relevant academic certificates or degrees
on their profile. Look for the "CNIC Verified" badge for peace of
mind, especially since tuition involves regular, ongoing home visits.

WHAT TO DISCUSS BEFORE BOOKING
Before confirming a booking, it helps to agree with the home teacher
on the exact subjects/grade level, weekly schedule, and session
duration, since these details affect the monthly rate.

BOOKING A HOME TEACHER
Search for "Home Teachers" under service categories, filter by city
and subject if mentioned in your search, and compare verified home
teacher profiles by rating and price before scheduling.
