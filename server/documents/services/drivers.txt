HelpGhar - Drivers

WHAT DRIVERS OFFER
HelpGhar drivers provide personal driving services for daily commutes,
school runs, errands, and long-distance trips. Many are experienced
with both manual and automatic vehicles and are familiar with major
city routes.

ARRANGEMENT TYPES
Drivers can typically be booked on a monthly basis for regular daily
duty, or by the hour/day for occasional trips. Check the pricing unit
listed on each driver's profile.

WHAT TO EXPECT
- Punctual pickup and drop-off
- Familiarity with local routes and traffic patterns
- Safe, defensive driving practices
- Basic vehicle care awareness (if driving the customer's own car)

LICENSING AND VERIFICATION
Drivers should hold a valid driving license appropriate to the vehicle
type. HelpGhar verifies each driver's CNIC as part of onboarding: look
for the CNIC-verified badge on their profile, and ask to see their
license details directly if you have specific requirements.

WHOSE VEHICLE IS USED
Some drivers only drive the customer's own vehicle, while others may
also offer their own car for hire - check the individual worker's
profile or ask before booking, since this affects pricing.

BOOKING A DRIVER
Search for "Drivers" under service categories, filter by city and
budget, and compare verified profiles by rating and experience before
scheduling.
