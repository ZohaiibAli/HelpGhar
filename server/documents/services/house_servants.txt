HelpGhar - House Servants

WHAT HOUSE SERVANTS DO
HelpGhar's House Servants category covers general domestic help for
daily household upkeep: sweeping, mopping, dusting, dishwashing, basic
laundry, and tidying living spaces. This is different from the
Cleaners category, which focuses on scheduled deep-cleaning visits -
House Servants are typically hired for ongoing, day-to-day help rather
than a one-off deep clean.

TYPICAL ARRANGEMENT
Most House Servants on HelpGhar are booked on a monthly basis for
regular daily or alternate-day visits, though some are available for
hourly or daily work as well. Check each worker's listed price unit
(hour, day, or month) on their profile.

WHAT'S TYPICALLY INCLUDED
- Sweeping and mopping floors
- Dusting and general tidying
- Dishwashing
- Basic laundry and folding
- Bed-making and room organising

WHAT'S USUALLY NOT INCLUDED
Cooking, childcare, and heavy-duty deep cleaning are usually separate
services (see Cooks, Baby Sitters, and Cleaners categories) unless a
specific worker's profile states they also offer those.

VERIFICATION
Look for the CNIC-verified badge on a worker's profile before hiring
someone for regular home access. HelpGhar's admin team reviews CNIC
and any submitted references as part of the verification process.

BOOKING A HOUSE SERVANT
Search for "House Servants" under service categories, filter by city
and budget, and compare verified profiles by rating and experience
before booking.
