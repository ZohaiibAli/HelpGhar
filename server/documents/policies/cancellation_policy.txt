HelpGhar - Cancellation Policy

CUSTOMER CANCELLATIONS
Customers may cancel a confirmed booking free of charge up to 2 hours
before the scheduled service start time. Cancellations made after this
2-hour cutoff may incur a late cancellation charge, since the worker has
already reserved that time slot and may have turned down other work.

HOW TO CANCEL
Cancellations can be made from the "My Bookings" section of the customer
dashboard. Once cancelled, the system automatically releases the worker's
time slot so it becomes available to other customers.

WORKER NOTIFICATION
Workers are notified immediately whenever a customer cancels or reschedules
a booking, so they can plan the rest of their day accordingly.

RESCHEDULING INSTEAD OF CANCELLING
If a customer needs to change the date or time rather than cancel entirely,
rescheduling is available up to the same 2-hour cutoff, subject to the
worker's availability for the new slot.

LATE CANCELLATION CHARGES
Charges for cancelling after the 2-hour window are intended to compensate
the worker for the reserved time and are disclosed to the customer before
the cancellation is confirmed. Charges vary depending on the service
category and the amount of notice given.

WORKER-INITIATED CANCELLATIONS
If a worker cancels a confirmed booking, the customer is notified
immediately and is not charged. Repeated cancellations by a worker may
result in a warning or account review by the admin team.

CANCELLATIONS DUE TO EMERGENCIES
HelpGhar reviews cancellation disputes on a case-by-case basis. If a
cancellation was due to a genuine emergency on either side, customers or
workers can raise the issue through the dispute form for a fair review.
