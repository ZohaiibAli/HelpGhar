HelpGhar - Tutoring Services

WHAT TUTORS OFFER
HelpGhar tutors offer home tuition across school and college levels,
including Matric, O-Levels, A-Levels, and general subject tutoring such as
Mathematics, Science, English, and Computer Studies. Tutor profiles list
their subject specialisation and experience so you can match a tutor to
your child's exact needs.

HOW HOME TUTORING WORKS
Once you book a tutor, sessions are typically scheduled on a recurring
basis (for example, a fixed number of days per week), with the price shown
on the tutor's profile usually reflecting a per-month or per-hour rate,
depending on how the tutor has listed it.

COMPARING TUTOR RATES
Rates vary based on subject, grade level, and the tutor's experience and
qualifications. O-Level and A-Level tutors, or subject specialists (e.g.
Mathematics, Physics), are generally priced higher than general primary or
middle-school tuition. Always compare a few verified profiles by rating,
experience, and price range before choosing.

VERIFICATION FOR TUTORS
Tutors go through the same CNIC verification as other HelpGhar workers,
and many also list relevant academic certificates or degrees on their
profile. Look for the "CNIC Verified" badge for peace of mind, especially
since tuition involves regular, ongoing home visits.

WHAT TO DISCUSS BEFORE BOOKING
Before confirming a booking, it helps to agree with the tutor on the exact
subjects/grade level, weekly schedule, and session duration, since these
details affect the monthly rate.

BOOKING A TUTOR
Search for "Tutor" under service categories, filter by city and subject if
mentioned in your search, and compare verified tutor profiles by rating
and price before scheduling.
